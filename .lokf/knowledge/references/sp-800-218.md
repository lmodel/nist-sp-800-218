# NIST SP 800-218, SSDF version 1.1

NIST Special Publication (SP) 800-218 is titled "Secure Software Development Framework (SSDF) Version 1.1: Recommendations for Mitigating the Risk of Software Vulnerabilities". NIST's page for it gives the date published as February 2022, the authors as Murugiah Souppaya, Karen Scarfone and Donna Dodson, and the document history as a draft on 30 September 2021 and the final on 3 February 2022.[^final] It supersedes NIST Cybersecurity White Paper 13 of April 2020, which the project page says defined SSDF version 1.0.[^ssdf] The page offers the SSDF table as a spreadsheet beside the publication. The publication is at <https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.SP.800-218>.

SP 800-218 Revision 1, titled "Secure Software Development Framework (SSDF) Version 1.2", was published as an initial public draft on 17 December 2025, and its comment period closed on 30 January 2026.[^ipd] The README says that the schema follows final editions, so it does not model the draft.[^readme.md]

`scripts/check_upstream_release.py` asks doi.org every week whether NIST has registered `10.6028/NIST.SP.800-218r1`, the identifier the next final edition would take, and reports the draft identifiers it finds for information only.[^check_upstream_release.py] [NIST's SSDF catalog copy](../datasets/ssdf-catalog-copy.md) is the machine-readable form of this edition that the repository keeps.
