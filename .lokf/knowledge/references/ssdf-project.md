# NIST's SSDF project page

NIST's project page for the Secure Software Development Framework says that the SSDF's practices are organized into four groups: Prepare the Organization (PO), Protect the Software (PS), Produce Well-Secured Software (PW) and Respond to Vulnerabilities (RV).[^ssdf] It says that each practice is defined with four elements:

- the **practice**: its name and a unique identifier, then a short explanation of what it is and why it helps;
- the **task**: an action that may be needed to perform a practice;
- the **notional implementation example**: a notional example of the types of tools, processes or other methods that could help implement a task, where no example is required and the examples are not the only options;
- the **reference**: a pointer to an established secure development practice document and its mappings to a particular task.

The page also says that NIST has finalized SP 800-218A, a community profile of the SSDF for generative artificial intelligence and dual-use foundation models, and that it lists other community profiles. The page was created on 25 February 2021 and updated on 6 October 2026. The schema gives each element a class, as [the glossary](../glossary/index.md) says.
