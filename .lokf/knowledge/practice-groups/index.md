# Practice-groups

* [Prepare the Organization](prepare-the-organization.md) - The SSDF practice group PO, Prepare the Organization: the organization readies its people, processes and technology for secure software development. It holds the practices PO.1 to PO.5 and thirteen tasks.
* [Protect Software](protect-software.md) - The SSDF practice group PS, Protect Software: the organization protects every component of its software from tampering and unauthorized access. It holds the practices PS.1 to PS.3 and four tasks.
* [Produce Well-Secured Software](produce-well-secured-software.md) - The SSDF practice group PW, Produce Well-Secured Software: the organization produces software with few security vulnerabilities in its releases. It holds eight practices, PW.1, PW.2 and PW.4 to PW.9, and sixteen tasks.
* [Respond to Vulnerabilities](respond-to-vulnerabilities.md) - The SSDF practice group RV, Respond to Vulnerabilities: the organization finds the vulnerabilities left in its releases, responds to them and prevents similar ones. It holds the practices RV.1 to RV.3 and nine tasks.
