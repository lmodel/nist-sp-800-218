# Respond to Vulnerabilities (RV)

The catalog's overview of the group says that an organization should identify residual vulnerabilities in its software releases, respond to address them, and prevent similar vulnerabilities from occurring in the future.[^nist_sp800-218_ver1_catalog.json] The group holds 3 practices and 9 tasks in SSDF version 1.1:

- `RV.1` Identify and Confirm Vulnerabilities on an Ongoing Basis: 3 tasks
- `RV.2` Assess, Prioritize, and Remediate Vulnerabilities: 2 tasks
- `RV.3` Analyze Vulnerabilities to Identify Their Root Causes: 4 tasks
